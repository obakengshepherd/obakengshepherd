## 🧠 How I Think About Analytics

I try to keep the process simple:

**1. What is the actual question?**

**2. What data do we have?**

**3. Can we trust it?**

**4. What does the analysis actually show?**

**5. Why does it matter?**

**6. What should someone do with the information?**

The final step matters.

**Insight without action is just an interesting spreadsheet.**

---

## 🔬 My Working Philosophy

* Start with the business question, not the tool.
* Understand the data before drawing conclusions.
* Make assumptions visible.
* Separate facts from interpretation.
* Prefer clear analysis over unnecessary complexity.
* Build reproducible work where possible.
* Communicate findings in plain language.
* Treat dashboards as decision-support tools.
* Use technology to solve problems, not to decorate them.

And, whenever possible:

> **If the data says something interesting, let the data do the talking.**
